# Heart of the Progression 1.0.0

First public release of **Heart of the Progression**, consolidating the previous development builds. Former 1.8.4 is now 1.0.0; earlier builds use 0.x.x numbers. The technical mod ID, save keys and gameplay balance remain compatible.

- Separate RPG experience from mining, combat, fishing, cooking, smelting, sprinting and fast swimming. First advancement costs 500 XP; growth is `round(500 × (91/90)^(L−1))`. No gameplay level cap.
- Six attributes starting at 10, one attribute point per level, one skill point at levels 5, 10, 15… and one point per skill unlock.
- Inventory expansion through Endurance, Intelligence-based recipes and configurable item-use requirements.
- Shared stamina, exhaustion penalties, a regenerating spark reservoir, dimensional travel costs and milk-resistant Spark Exhaustion.
- Hunger replaced with passive healing and food bonuses for protection, movement, attacks, stamina and spark recovery. Golden apple bonuses replace their vanilla effects.
- Physical, elemental and magical protection, burning, poisoning and exhaustion protection. Vanilla armor contributes physically; Iron’s armor contributes physically and magically at half armor/toughness values.
- Optional ParCool and Epic Fight skill trees, shared resources, disabled Epic Fight skill books and weapon-dependent attack costs.
- Optional Iron’s mana from 0 to 8, unlocked through Spark Potential, with spell costs converted at 25:1. Scrolls and weapon spells consume mana; equipment mana bonuses are disabled, while natural recovery and instant mana potions remain.
- Custom menus, descriptions, center-filled HUD meters, conditional oxygen/stamina/mount health, a temporary vanilla XP display and 0.4-second HUD transitions.
- Top-left mana ring with the selected spell icon, shown with an equipped Curios spellbook or a held scroll. Native permanent Iron’s mana/spell displays are hidden.
- Fixes for texture overlap, player-removal crashes, shared-resource synchronization, restricted-slot/crafting bypasses and duplicate cooking rewards.
- Configurable balance, data-pack recipe requirements, administrative commands, Russian/English localization and persistent character progression.

The own-mod and Iron’s skill tabs are reserved for future skills. Install the same release on client and server. Updating preserves progression; the existing config migration changes the former standard XP base of 100 to 500.
